## English (en-US)

**App name:** Tour-op

**Short description** (71 characters)

Meet trusted locals in Vietnam. Agree on price and plans before you go.

**Full description**

Travelling alone or in a small group? Tour-op connects you with locals who know their city, from food and sightseeing to activities, and helps you agree on everything before you meet.

Tour-op is built around the worries most travellers have about going out with a stranger.

WORRIED ABOUT BEING OVERCHARGED?
The price, local fees and any possible extra costs are agreed by both of you before the tour is confirmed.

WORRIED ABOUT BEING TAKEN SOMEWHERE YOU DIDN'T WANT TO GO?
The itinerary is agreed in advance. Shopping and partner stops are clearly marked, and you can decline them.

NOT SURE THE PERSON IS WHO THEY SAY THEY ARE?
Profiles show separate, honest signals: verified email and phone, manual checks, and reviews that only come from real bookings.

DON'T WANT TO SHARE YOUR PHONE NUMBER?
Chat inside the app. Your email and phone number are never shown to other members unless you choose to share them.

HOW IT WORKS
• Find locals who match your interests, languages, dates and budget
• Or post a trip wish and let suitable locals come to you
• Chat in the app and agree on the price and itinerary together
• Get notified when someone replies or your booking changes
• After the tour, both sides leave a review

STAYING SAFE
• Share your trip details with family or friends
• Report or block anyone, at any time
• Agreements and messages are kept, so problems can be looked into fairly

FOR LOCALS
Know your city? Create a profile, publish the experiences you offer and when you're available, and read each traveller's profile and request before deciding to meet.

Tour-op is currently available in Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang and Hai Phong, in English and Vietnamese. You must be 18 or older to use Tour-op.

No platform can guarantee complete safety. Use your judgement, and report anything that feels wrong.
